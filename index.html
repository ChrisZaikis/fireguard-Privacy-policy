Privacy & Data Handling

Privacy Policy

Applies to FireGuard on iOS, Android, web pages and related services.

This Privacy Policy explains how FireGuard (the “App”) collects, uses, stores and shares data when you use the App. The App is designed as an informational civil-protection and emergency-safety tool. It does not replace official emergency services, civil protection authorities, emergency communications, medical advice, professional judgment or official evacuation instructions.

1. Summary

  • No third-party advertising currently displayed: the App does not currently display third-party advertising banners, interstitial ads or advertising-network placements.
  • No sale of personal data: we do not sell personal data.
  • Location is optional: the App may request location permission for local information, map functions, alerts and emergency-related tools.
  • Notifications are optional: the App may use local and remote push notifications if enabled by the user.
  • Emergency information is user-controlled: emergency profile, emergency contact or medical-related fields are provided voluntarily by the user.

Emergency safety notice: In a real emergency, immediately contact the competent emergency authority, follow official instructions and do not rely only on the App. The App may contain delays, inaccuracies, missing data, service interruptions, device-level limitations or third-party data errors.

2. Data We May Collect and Why

  • Location data
  • Emergency profile data
  • Notification data
  • Device and diagnostics data
  • Account or support data
  • Public-data and map interaction data

3. Advertising Status

The App does not currently display third-party advertising. If third-party advertising is introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated before or at the time of such introduction.

4. Location Data

Location may be used to provide map, alert and local-information features. Users may deny, limit or revoke location permission at any time through device settings.

5. Notifications

If notifications are enabled, the App may use Apple Push Notification service, Firebase Cloud Messaging and local device notifications. Notification delivery is not guaranteed.

6. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

7. Your Rights

Depending on jurisdiction, users may have rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability and withdrawal of consent.
